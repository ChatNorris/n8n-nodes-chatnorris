# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Primera versión / Initial release.

- Credencial `ChatNorris API` (Bearer, `baseUrl` configurable).
- Nodo `ChatNorris`: Chatbot (Get, Get Many), Message (Send, con adjunto opcional), Conversation (Get, Get Many, Get Messages), Lead (Get, Get Many), Knowledge Source (Get Many, Add, Delete, Resync).
- Nodo `ChatNorris Trigger` (polling): New Lead y New Conversation, con filtro opcional por agente.
